Three pieces each, set out in a spiral. On your turn, turn a ring or step a piece. A turn carries every piece on that ring, the other side's too. First to line up three wins.

A field of two-sided mirrors and two beams of light. On your turn, flip one mirror that a beam passes through. Every flip can bend both beams. First to bring its own light home wins.
